Privacy Policy for the Genzora mobile application

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and your choices regarding your information.

2. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide shopping services.
  • Process and manage orders.
  • Deliver products.
  • Process applicable payments.
  • Manage cancellations, returns and refunds.
  • Maintain cart and wishlist functionality.
  • Send important order and service notifications.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Improve the App and user experience.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, misuse and security issues.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

3. Firebase Services

Genzora uses Firebase services to support features such as authentication and data storage. Depending on the features you use, information may be processed through services such as Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.

4. Notifications

Genzora may send notifications related to your account, orders, payment status, delivery status or other important app activity. You may control notification permissions through your device settings or available App settings.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared when necessary to provide requested services, process payments, fulfill orders, operate required third-party services, protect the App, comply with legal obligations, or prevent fraud and misuse.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain account and transaction records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations and fulfill legitimate business purposes.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including requesting access, correction or deletion of certain information.

You may also control certain permissions, such as notifications, through your device or App settings.

9. Children's Privacy

Genzora is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

10. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services required for authentication, data storage, payments, notifications, security and other functionality. These services may process information according to their applicable privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App, our services or applicable legal requirements. When changes are made, the effective date will be updated.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Genzora, please contact us through the official contact information associated with the Genzora application.
